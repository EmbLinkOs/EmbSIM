# rc-charge — analytic

The capacitor starts discharged (`ic: 0`; without it the operating point would
charge C1 to 5 V since it is open at DC). With τ = R·C = 1 ms:

    v_out(t) = 5 · (1 − e^(−t/τ))

    t = 0.5 ms: 1.9673467 V
    t = 1 ms:   3.1606028 V   (63.2 %)
    t = 2 ms:   4.3233236 V
    t = 3 ms:   4.7510643 V
    t = 10 ms:  4.9997730 V

Tolerance 1 % relative plus 2 mV: the trapezoidal integrator with LTE control
at `lte_tol` 1e-3 stays well inside. Generated by hand.
