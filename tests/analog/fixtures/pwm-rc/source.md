# pwm-rc — analytic (periodic steady state)

A 1 kHz, 50 % PWM of 0/3.3 V into R = 1 kΩ, C = 100 nF (τ = 100 µs, so each
half period is 5 τ). In periodic steady state the voltage at the end of the
high half and the end of the low half are

    v_high = 3.3 · (1 − e^(−5)) / (1 − e^(−10)) = 3.27779 V
    v_low  = v_high · e^(−5)                      = 0.02208 V

reached to within 1e-4 after a few periods (the transient from 0 decays as
e^(−10) per period). The mean is 1.65 V and the ripple v_high − v_low.
Generated by hand.
